about=Features:
  - Resampling & raster value extraction inside a boundary.
  - PCA with explained variance table (incl. Eigenvalue λ) and CSV export.
  - K-Means clustering with Elbow curve and Silhouette score; export indices; generate zones as a raster.
  - Optional zone smoothing (Mode Filter): SAGA Majority/Minority.
  - Variance Reduction analysis from zones and external points CSV; per-zone statistics and boxplots (PNG).

  Requirements:
  - Python packages: pandas, scikit-learn (optional: scipy).
  - QGIS "Processing SAGA NextGen Provider" enabled.
